LOGO ASSETS
===========
logo.svg is the animated mark used in the hero section.

To replace it:
1. Drop your own file in here (SVG recommended, PNG/WebP also fine).
2. Update `logo.path` in /config/site.config.js to point at the new filename.
3. If your logo is a raster image (PNG/WebP), also set `logo.isRaster: true`
   in the config so the iris-reveal animation adjusts correctly.

Recommended size: square, at least 240x240px, transparent background.
